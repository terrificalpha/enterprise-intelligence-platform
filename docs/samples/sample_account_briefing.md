# Sample account briefing (C0085)

**Summary**
C0085 (Manufacturing, SMB, ACV $20,500) has a predicted churn probability of 100%, which places it in the **High Risk** tier. Expected revenue at risk is about $20,402.
Policy definition of the tiers: "High Risk: predicted churn probability of 0.60 or higher." [1]

**Why this account is at risk**
- **SLA breaches (90d)** = 3 raises churn risk by 1.6 pts
- **NPS score** = 2 raises churn risk by 1.5 pts
- **Competitor mentions (90d)** = 2 raises churn risk by 1.3 pts
- **Contract type** = Monthly raises churn risk by 0.9 pts

**Recommended actions**
1. Start the High-Risk save plan: executive outreach call, written save plan approved by the Regional VP - triggered by predicted High Risk. Policy: "A written save plan must be approved by the Regional VP within 10 business days." [2]
2. Trigger executive escalation and deliver a root cause analysis - triggered by 3 SLA breaches in 90 days. Policy: "Three or more SLA breaches in a rolling 90-day window trigger an executive escalation and a root cause analysis delivered within 7 days." [3]
3. Offer a training and enablement plan and run a customer health review - triggered by seat utilization 34%, usage trend -16.3%. Policy: "When seat utilization falls below 50 percent, a training and enablement plan must be offered within 30 days." [4]
4. Prepare a competitive defence (value-based, multi-year terms rather than deep discounts) - triggered by 2 competitor mentions. Policy: "Defend at-risk accounts with value-based selling and multi-year terms rather than deep discounts." [5]
5. Add to the Head of Support monthly review (resolution time above the 24h target) - triggered by avg resolution 44h. Policy: "Customers with an average resolution time above 24 hours are reviewed monthly by the Head of Support." [6]
